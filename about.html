About Me

I'm Alex Chen, a product designer passionate about creating meaningful digital experiences that make a difference in people's lives. With over 5 years of experience, I've helped startups and established companies craft products that users love.

My approach combines strategic thinking, user research, and beautiful aesthetics to solve complex problems with simple, elegant solutions. I believe great design is invisible—it just works.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me exploring photography, reading design books, or mentoring aspiring designers in my community.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about my work and process

My design process follows a user-centered approach starting with research and discovery, moving through ideation and prototyping, and culminating in testing and iteration. I believe in collaborative design and involve stakeholders throughout the process to ensure alignment and buy-in.
I primarily use Figma for UI/UX design and prototyping, Adobe Creative Suite for graphic design work, and various other tools like Miro for collaboration, Notion for documentation, and Webflow for no-code development when appropriate.
Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A simple website redesign might take 4–6 weeks, while a comprehensive product design including research, design system, and multiple platforms could take 3–6 months. I always provide detailed timelines during the proposal phase.
Absolutely! I work with clients globally and have established effective remote collaboration processes using tools like Zoom, Figma, and Loom. Clear communication and regular check-ins ensure successful outcomes regardless of location.
I combine strategic thinking with creative execution, always grounding my work in data and user research. I don't just make things look pretty—I solve business problems through thoughtful design that drives measurable results.
While my primary focus is design, I have a strong understanding of front-end development and can provide design systems and specifications that make handoff seamless. I also have a network of trusted developers I can recommend for implementation.
